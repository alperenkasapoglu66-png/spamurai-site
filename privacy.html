Spamurai Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

In short: Spamurai collects no data. The app and its message filter have no network access; your messages never leave your device.

What Spamurai does

Spamurai is an SMS filter for iOS. It examines SMS messages from senders who are not in your contacts, on your device, and moves the ones it identifies as gambling or betting promotions or scam attempts to the junk folder of the Messages app.

Your messages

  • iOS gives the filter extension only SMS and MMS messages from senders who are not in your contacts. Messages from your contacts and iMessage conversations never reach the extension.
  • The sender and text of a message are evaluated in your device's memory at that moment only. Spamurai does not save, store or log messages.
  • The filter extension does not connect to the internet. No message, sender information or filtering result is sent to us or to any third party.
  • iOS does not allow the filter extension to write to the storage it shares with the app, so the app cannot see your filtered messages.

What is stored on your device

Only the settings you enter are stored: whether the filter is on, the sensitivity level, blocked words, allowed words and trusted senders. These settings stay on your device and are not sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

Text you paste into the app's test screen is evaluated on screen only and is not saved.

What we do not collect

Spamurai has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking technology and no third-party software libraries. We do not collect, process, sell or share personal data.

What Apple may collect

If you have turned on "Share With App Developers" on your device, Apple may provide the developer with crash and usage statistics that do not identify you. For TestFlight builds Apple may collect similar diagnostic data. That data is covered by Apple's privacy policy and does not include your message content.

Children

Because Spamurai collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the current text will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
